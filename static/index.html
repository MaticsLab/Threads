How it works

Artwork: every stroke narrower than the satin cap is stitched as a true satin column on the stroke's perpendicular; anything wider gets a tatami, contour or circular fill underneath. The finished file is measured — coverage, gaps, stitch lengths, hoop clearance — and rebuilt until it passes.

Lettering: text is sewn from Ink/Stitch embroidery fonts with Ink/Stitch's rail-and-rung satin engine. Import: SVGs are digitized like Inkscape files; machine files (DST, PES, …) convert to any other format.

Preview modes include a lit realistic view and full 3D. Thread brands are matched by palette — add your own brand's colour card with ＋ Brand.
